Free

Everything for everyday PDFs.

Viewing, annotations, page management, and sharing are included at no cost. Every annotation is saved automatically as you go.

View

Open PDFs from the Files app, Mail, or other apps. Smooth scrolling even in long documents, a page overview with thumbnails, and the app remembers the last page you read.

Annotate

Pen for finger and Apple Pencil, highlighter, text notes, rectangle, ellipse, and arrow. Move, resize, copy, and paste annotations — with undo and redo.

Pages & Sharing

Delete, reorder, and rotate pages. Share PDFs with all annotations via Mail, Messages, AirDrop, or the Files app.

Premium

More than just reading.

Premium is available as a monthly subscription, a yearly subscription, or a one-time purchase. Both subscriptions come with a free one-week trial.

Sign

Draw your signature by hand, save it, and reuse it in any document — place it, move it, and resize it.

Merge

Combine several PDFs into one document in the order you choose. The originals stay unchanged.

Watermark

Text or image watermarks with position, opacity, rotation, and size — on all pages or only selected ones.

Compress

Shrink PDFs with three levels. Documents with many images benefit the most. The original is kept.

Share without the note

Shared PDFs without the subtle “Created with Eunexon PDF” note.
