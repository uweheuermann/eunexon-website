2. Principle: No data collection

Eunexon PDF does not collect any personal data and does not transmit any data to the developer’s servers or to third parties. The app requires no account, does not connect to the internet on its own, and uses no advertising, no tracking technologies, and no analytics SDKs.

3. Documents and content on your device

PDFs, annotations, saved signatures, watermark settings, and the document list are stored only on your device — in the app’s container or in the file you opened yourself. The developer has no access to them.

When you open a PDF from the Files app or another location, the app only remembers a reference to that specific file so it can reopen it later and save changes back to it. The app does not access any other files. Depending on your system settings, app data may be included in a device or iCloud backup managed by Apple; if a file is stored in iCloud Drive, Apple syncs it like any other file there. The developer has no access to such backups or storage locations.

4. Photos for image watermarks

For an image watermark, you choose a photo using the system photo picker. The app receives only the selected image and has no access to the rest of your photo library. The image is processed only on your device.

5. In-app purchases and subscriptions

Premium can be unlocked with a monthly subscription, a yearly subscription, or a one-time purchase. Offers, payment, free trials, renewals, cancellations, and restoring purchases are handled by Apple and the App Store. The developer does not receive any credit card, bank, or other payment data. The app only uses Apple StoreKit to check whether Premium is unlocked and stores this status locally.

For more information, see Apple’s App Store & Privacy notice.

6. Sharing documents

When you share a document, the app opens the system share sheet. You choose the service and the recipient yourself. The developer receives no information about whether, how, or with whom documents are shared.

7. Links

The links to the Terms of Use and to this Privacy Policy open in your browser. The privacy notices of the respective provider apply to the websites you visit; for this website, see the Website Privacy Policy.

8. Retention and deletion

You can remove locally stored app data by deleting documents in the app or by deleting the app from your device. Files in other locations that you opened in the app remain there. App Store purchase data and any backups are managed by Apple.

9. Your rights and contact

Because the developer does not store any personal data from the app on its own systems, no user-related records exist there. Questions about privacy can be sent to support@eunexon.com.

10. Changes

This Privacy Policy will be updated if the app’s features, the services used, or legal requirements change.
