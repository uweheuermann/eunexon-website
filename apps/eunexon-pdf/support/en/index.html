Quick guide in the app

In the document list, tap the question mark. The quick guide explains every tool — from annotating and managing pages to the Premium features.

Restore purchases

In Eunexon PDF, open a Premium feature such as Watermark and choose “Restore Purchases” on the Premium screen. Use the same Apple Account you used to buy Premium.

Manage or cancel a subscription

You manage subscriptions in the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad: tap your name, then Subscriptions. A subscription renews automatically unless you cancel it at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. The free trial week turns into a paid subscription unless you cancel. The one-time purchase is not a subscription and does not renew.

Frequently asked questions

Where are my documents stored?

Only on your device: in the app’s storage or wherever the file you opened is located — for example in the Files app or in iCloud Drive. Eunexon PDF does not upload anything to external servers.

Why do shared PDFs say “Created with Eunexon PDF”?

Without Premium, the shared copy carries this subtle note at the bottom of each page. With Premium, it’s removed. The original file and the view in the app never show the note.

Can a watermark be removed?

No. A watermark is written permanently into the file. If you want to keep the original, choose “Save as New File” when adding it.
